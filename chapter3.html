Chapter 3

Three Wishes

Aladdin learns the rules of the lamp, escapes the cave, and decides to become Prince Ali.

As the enormous blue figure floated above him, Aladdin struggled to believe what he was seeing. The Genie of the Lamp radiated magical power, yet his cheerful personality made him seem far less frightening than his size suggested. After introducing himself, the Genie explained that the lamp's owner was entitled to three wishes. Although the magic was powerful enough to change lives and kingdoms, there were limits to what it could do. The Genie could not force someone to fall in love, bring the dead back to life, or grant additional wishes. Apart from those restrictions, however, almost anything seemed possible.

Aladdin quickly realised that his most urgent problem was escaping the Cave of Wonders. Surrounded by collapsing stone and trapped beneath the desert, he used his first wish to return safely to the surface.

As Aladdin and the Genie travelled back towards Agrabah, his thoughts became increasingly complicated. He could not stop thinking about Jasmine and became convinced that someone like her could never be interested in a poor street thief.

Was he truly unworthy of Jasmine, or had he simply spent his entire life believing that wealth determined a person's value?

Using his second wish, he asked the Genie to transform him into a wealthy and impressive prince known throughout the kingdom as Prince Ali.
