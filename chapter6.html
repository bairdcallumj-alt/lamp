Chapter 6

Jafar's Rise

Jafar seizes the lamp, takes the throne, and transforms Agrabah into a kingdom ruled by fear.

Once the lamp was in his possession, Jafar wasted no time putting its power to use. Having spent years serving beside the Sultan while secretly craving greater authority, he finally possessed the means to turn ambition into reality. Summoning the Genie, he used his first wish to place himself upon the throne of Agrabah. In an instant, the balance of power within the kingdom changed. The Sultan's authority vanished, his crown was stripped away, and Jafar assumed control of the palace.

For many citizens, the change was confusing at first. Jafar presented himself as a strong and capable ruler who would bring order and prosperity to the kingdom. Beneath the polished speeches and promises, however, lay a very different reality. Unlike the Sultan, whose greatest weakness had often been his kindness, Jafar viewed power as something to be controlled rather than shared. He valued obedience above loyalty and fear above respect.

Unsatisfied with simply becoming ruler of Agrabah, Jafar soon used a second wish to transform himself into the most powerful sorcerer in the world. Magical energy surged through the palace as his powers expanded beyond anything an ordinary human could possess. With a wave of his staff, he could alter the appearance of buildings, command the elements, and bend others to his will. The kingdom that had once been governed by laws now found itself controlled by the whims of a single individual.

Jasmine refused to accept Jafar's rule. She openly challenged his decisions and reminded the people of Agrabah that power gained through fear could never create a just kingdom. Her defiance only strengthened Jafar's determination to silence her. Using his magic, he imprisoned Jasmine within the palace and ensured that every attempt to resist his authority would be swiftly punished.

As Jafar's control tightened, life throughout Agrabah began to change. Markets grew quieter, citizens became fearful of speaking openly, and palace guards enforced the sorcerer's commands without question. The city that had once been filled with colour, energy, and possibility gradually became overshadowed by suspicion and fear. While Jafar enjoyed the power he had spent years pursuing, the people of Agrabah paid the price, and many began to wonder whether anyone remained capable of stopping him before the kingdom was changed forever.
