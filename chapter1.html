Chapter 1

A Princess in Disguise

Jasmine leaves the palace, Aladdin is captured in the marketplace, and Jafar sees the chance he has been waiting for.

The city of Agrabah sparkled beneath the desert sun, its golden domes and white stone towers rising above a maze of crowded streets filled with merchants, travellers, and market stalls. From the highest balcony of the Sultan's palace, Princess Jasmine could see the entire city stretching towards the horizon, yet despite the magnificent view, she felt trapped. Every day seemed to follow the same routine of lessons, ceremonies, and meetings with visiting princes, all of whom appeared far more interested in her title than her thoughts, dreams, or ambitions. As her next birthday approached, the pressure to choose a husband intensified, making the palace feel less like a home and more like a cage decorated with marble and gold.

Determined to experience life beyond the palace walls, Jasmine disguised herself in simple clothing and slipped unnoticed into the city. For the first time in years, she found herself surrounded by ordinary people rather than servants and advisers. The bustling marketplace fascinated her. Merchants argued over prices, children darted through the crowd, and performers entertained audiences with music and acrobatics. The city felt alive in a way the palace never had.

Among the crowd moved a young thief named Aladdin, whose daily struggle for survival had taught him to think quickly and act even faster. Driven by hunger, he stole a loaf of bread from a baker's stall and disappeared into the marketplace, only to attract the attention of the palace guards. During the chase, he briefly encountered Jasmine and was struck by how differently she carried herself from everyone around her. Although neither revealed their true identity, both left the encounter curious about the other.

The chase ended when the guards finally captured Aladdin and dragged him away toward the palace prison. As he disappeared into the crowd, Jasmine found herself wondering what circumstances had driven him to steal in the first place. Watching from a shadowed palace balcony, however, another observer had taken a far greater interest. Jafar, the Sultan's ambitious adviser, believed he had finally found the person capable of entering the legendary Cave of Wonders.
