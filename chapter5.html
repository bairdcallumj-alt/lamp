Chapter 5

The Truth

A magic carpet ride deepens Jasmine's trust, but Aladdin's secret becomes harder to hide as Jafar closes in on the lamp.

As the days passed, Aladdin found it increasingly difficult to separate his real self from the identity he had created. Although the title of Prince Ali had opened palace doors and earned the respect of nobles, it was during his time with Jasmine that he felt most like himself. One evening, hoping to escape the expectations and formality that surrounded them, he invited her on a journey aboard a magical flying carpet.

Together they soared above Agrabah as the city lights flickered below like scattered stars. They drifted across deserts, mountains, and moonlit seas, watching the world unfold beneath them. Free from palace walls and royal responsibilities, Jasmine appeared happier than Aladdin had ever seen her. For the first time, she felt that someone truly listened to her thoughts rather than simply hearing her title. As the carpet carried them through the night sky, a genuine bond began to form between them.

The experience strengthened Jasmine's trust in Aladdin, yet it had the opposite effect on him. The closer they became, the more uncomfortable he felt about the secret he continued to hide. He knew that Jasmine admired qualities such as honesty and independence, and he worried that revealing the truth would destroy everything they had built together. Despite these concerns, he repeatedly convinced himself that there would be time to explain later.

While Jasmine's trust was growing, Jafar's suspicions were growing even faster.

The royal adviser had spent years studying people and uncovering secrets, and something about Prince Ali refused to make sense. Determined to discover the truth, he began investigating the mysterious prince's sudden appearance. Before long, his search revealed a connection between Prince Ali and the street thief who had vanished from the palace prison only days earlier. More importantly, Jafar discovered the existence of the magical lamp.

Driven by his hunger for power, Jafar acted quickly. Under the cover of darkness, he stole the lamp from Aladdin's chambers and claimed it for himself. By the time Aladdin realised what had happened, the most powerful object in the kingdom was no longer in his possession, and the future he had imagined was beginning to collapse around him.
